This portfolio is designed to look like a computer desktop.
You do not need to install anything or create an account.


1. HOW TO OPEN IT
-----------------
1. If you received a .zip file, right-click it and choose
   "Extract All" (Windows) or double-click it (Mac).
2. Open the extracted folder named "portfolio".
3. Double-click the file "index.html".
   It opens in your web browser.

Tips:
- Use a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
- A laptop or desktop screen works best. On a phone, turn
  it sideways (landscape).
- Press F11 (Windows) for full screen.
- Keep all files together in the folder. Do not move
  index.html out of it, or the pictures will not show.
- An internet connection is only needed for the links that
  open Google Drive, Facebook or the landing page.


TWO VERSIONS OF THIS PORTFOLIO
------------------------------
index.html        - the creative "desktop" version (folders and dock).
traditional.html  - the classic website version (menu bar at the
                    top, scrolling sections, project cards).
Both have the same content, instructions window, and themes.
To switch, click the tab on the right edge of the screen
("Classic view" or "Desktop view"). Your chosen theme carries over.
In the classic version, scroll down or click the menu at the top.


2. HOW TO MOVE AROUND
---------------------
When the website opens, a short "How to use this portfolio"
window appears. Read it, then click the X to close it. You can
open it again anytime with the "Instructions" button at the
top right corner of the screen.

You can go to every page in two ways:

  A. The folders on the left side of the Home screen.
  B. The colorful icons in the dock at the bottom.
     Hover over an icon to see its name.

  Dock icons, from left to right:
    Notes     - click it, then choose Profile or About Me
    Safari    - Experience
    Camera    - Skills
    Photos    - Projects
    Calendar  - Certifications
    Messages  - Contact Me
    Heart     - Features
    Mail      - Thank You page

To go back to the Home screen from any page, click the small
house button on the far left edge of the screen.

4. VIEWING PROJECTS
-------------------
1. Open the Projects page (Photos icon or "projects" folder).
2. Click the black "View Projects" button.
3. Choose a project from the list.

On a project page:
- Click the small pictures on the left to see other images.
- Use "Next >" and "< Prev" on the pictures to move to the
  next or previous project.
- Click the menu (three lines) at the top right to jump to
  any project.
- Click the arrow at the top left to return to the Project
  Board.
- Click the link below the description to see the full
  project files. It opens in a new tab.


5. VIEWING CERTIFICATES
-----------------------
1. Open the Certifications page (Calendar icon).
2. Use the left and right arrows to browse one certificate
   at a time. The keyboard arrow keys also work.
3. Click "See all" (top right of the window) to view all the
   certificates at once. Scroll to browse.
4. Click any certificate to open it, or click "Back" to
   return.


6. FEATURES PAGE
----------------
Click the pictures to see the images and watch the videos.
Each one opens inside the window.
Click the round X button (top right of the window) to close
it and go back. Every preview also has an "Open in new tab"
button. If a preview looks blank, use that button, because
some websites do not allow previews.


7. CHANGING THE LOOK (THEMES)
-----------------------------
Click the small round button at the bottom right corner of
the screen. Choose one of:

  macOS       - the default desktop look
  Dark mode   - a darker version, easier on the eyes
  Mood board  - a purple and gold style based on the
                Guided Growth brand mood board

Your choice is remembered the next time you open the site.

9. TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------
- Pictures are missing:
    Make sure all the pictures (.jpg, .png) are in the same
    place as index.html, and that the "css" and "js" folders
    are next to it.
- Nothing happens when I click:
    Try another browser (Chrome or Edge) and make sure
    JavaScript is enabled.
- The page looks too small or too large:
    Resize the window or use full screen (F11).
- A link opens an error page:
    Check your internet connection or sign-in access for
    Google Drive and Facebook.
