How to use this portfolio

  1. Explore: click the folders on the Home screen or the icons in the dock. Hover over an icon to see its name.
  2. Notes icon: choose Profile or About me.
  3. Projects: open Projects, press View Projects, then pick a project.
  4. Certifications: use the arrows, or press See all.
  5. Features: click the pictures to see the images and watch the videos. Press × to close.
  6. Home: the house button on the left edge goes back to the Home screen.
  7. Theme: the round button at the bottom right switches between macOS, Dark mode and Mood board.

Close this with ×. You can open it again anytime from “Instructions” at the top right.
